## [Unreleased] — 2026-11-02 Per-patient referral rule and the whole-field simulator arm  (branch `retinareach`)

Why: on the first box run the simulator fooled the model's own camera probe (0.85 of simulated grade-0 photos called handheld) without inventing disease, but referable eyes lost signal (Canon model's sensitivity on simulated referable photos 0.87 -> 0.74), and the field stage keeps only ~65 % of a Canon FOV. Writes `predictions.csv` (`--from-predictions` reruns the rules without a GPU), `patient_rule.csv` and `patient_rule_summary.txt` (mean over seeds, seeds meeting the targets, paired difference of two arms; tabletop rows marked in-sample on `--part val`).
- `phone_sim.PhoneSimulator(field="whole")`, `train_retinareach.py --phone-sim-field {phone,whole}`, `style_transfer_xfer.py check/export --phone-field`, `phone_sim.py render --field` (ablation; default unchanged): the field stage keeps zoom 1 and no framing shift, so the whole tabletop FOV stays inside the aperture; un-squash, rotation, aperture and the disc bloom stay, and what lies outside a truncated FOV (its flat top and bottom) stays black through every stage. ## [Unreleased] — 2026-10-31 Phone-camera simulator: review fixes before the first box run  (branch `retinareach`)

A review of the 2026-10-30 change set (geometry, photometry, integration and leakage, label safety, GPU cost, tests), then a second review of these fixes; every finding above nit level was reproduced by a second check before it was fixed. REPORT.md §10.11.

- Training views (`phone_sim.TrainViews`, `PhoneSimulator.augment_views`). The trainer handed the simulator its RandomResizedCrop training view. A Canon FOV crop is 1.25:1 and the crop's width is capped at 1.1x its height, so the view cut the FOV on all four sides; `fov_shape` could not read its ellipse and the field stage fell back to the validation views' aspect, stretching every simulated training retina ~14 % horizontally (round dots came out 1.15 wide, 1.01 from the validation view that `fit` and `check` use) and cutting more nasal / temporal periphery. Now each source training photo yields two views from one decode: the ordinary training crop at the model's size and the whole FOV at the supersampled size. The simulator renders from the whole FOV; the photos it leaves alone keep the crop, so they no longer go 576 -> 384 in two resizes (which softened them like a ~0.4 px blur). The decode is drafted for the larger view, so for most BRSET frame sizes the kept crop comes from twice the Canon-only run's draft (mid-band energy +1.6 % median): the same transform on a slightly richer decode.
- Disc bloom confined to the disc (full to 0.85, gone by 1.1 detected radii; weight capped at 1). Reaching 1.3 radii with a weight steepest just above the FOV mean, it made peripapillary haemorrhages and exudates up to 2-4x more conspicuous (13-16 % of planted ones by more than 1.2x) and drew exudate-like flecks beside clean discs (an ICDR-0 sample).
- Ambiguous disc picks (`find_disc` `margin`, `disc_gate`, parameter `disc_margin` [1.2, 1.4]). On a PDR sample a pale laser-scar patch beat the disc on every training view by a margin of 1.05-1.17, with full confidence, and the bloom then painted a white disc over the lesions there. Below the margin the render's disc-keyed steps (bloom, shift toward the drawn photo's disc position, left-right mirroring) are switched off; true discs scored a median 4.9x (5th percentile 2.1). The bank's statistics do not use the gate.
- `fit` crashed on any GPU (`describe` mixed a CPU tensor into a device `torch.where`; the realism report called `.numpy()` on device tensors), i.e. on the box at its first batch; fixed, with a test on CUDA / MPS when one exists. On MPS, pooling sizes it cannot do (448, 512 px) fall back to the CPU, and `fov_mask` uses `amax` (`max(...).values` took up to a second per batch there).
- The warp prefilters before shrinking (blur 0.5 sqrt(step^2 - 1) px): bilinear sampling at ~1.5 source px per output px aliased texture and grain into crisper dots (planted microaneurysms +10 % peak depth).
- `strength` is limited to [0, 1] (simulator, `--phone-sim-strength`, `--phone-strength`, `render --strength`): above 1 the colour stage extrapolated whole channels to 0 and the bloom past the disc colour.
- Profile parameters are checked at load (`check_params`): a misspelt `colour_mode` silently ran the per-channel ablation; reversed `light_lim` clamped every gain to one value.
- Blotchiness (`illum_coeffs(with_residual=True)`): the residual is smoothed over the fitted cells only; cells outside the FOV and the left-out disc leaked in, so an evenly lit circle read 0.06-0.13. Profile format `phone_sim/2`: refit (no profile exists on the box yet).
- The shadow cap reads shadow on the FOV eroded as the bank's reading does (it read more shadow than the bank and scaled the light change back further than designed). The shrink target `src_illum` drops its left-right / up-down tilt (training flips the tabletop photos both ways).
- Aperture coverage is tested at the aperture's own edge (radius 1.0, 96 points; the zoom grid has 48 steps): testing at 0.97 left straight black chords inside the circle in nearly every render, a simulator-only cue.
- `geometry()` refuses a batch other than the last render's, and applies the aperture as the render does.
- Leakage guards. `fit` records the split, seed and stratification target (none for a folder of photos) and the bank's photo names. `train_retinareach.py --phone-sim` refuses a profile from another split, seed, stratification or handheld dataset, or one naming a photo outside its handheld train split (a validation / test photo, or a renamed copy), and records the profile (path, SHA-256, source, parameters) in `results.json` and the checkpoint. New `fit --strat-target` (the trainer's first source target). `fit --select icdr0` with `--target-dir`, and `--source-camera` with `--source-dir`, are refused (both were silently ignored). `style_transfer_xfer.py check` / `score` split on the checkpoint's own first source target, not always referable DR. `score --part val` scores the validation split of both cameras, where ablation arms are chosen (the test split is read once, for the chosen arm); its tabletop rows are marked in-sample (that split chose the epoch and fixed the threshold), their threshold columns left empty. `score_summary.txt` adds the per-seed paired difference of the two arms and each arm's checkpoint folder, phone_sim p, strength and profile hash (also columns of `score.csv`). `render` shows the validation split by default and writes to `ck_phone_sim/render`.
- `fit` report: real handheld photos outside the bank were dropped from the cross-fitted table (about half of them at the defaults) and a fold without one crashed; now they go to alternate folds, empty folds are left out, and the per-fold counts are printed. The tabletop column is read on a one-step model-size view. A FIELD line gives the zoom the renders actually got (on Canon frames the aperture's clearance sets it: median 1.31 on the samples against the fitted angular scale 1.10), how often the FOV ellipse was read, how often a dark FOV edge pushed the zoom past the fitted scale + 0.35, how often no zoom cleared the FOV, and how often the disc pick was ambiguous. The profile is saved before the report is built (a failing report no longer loses it); folds without bank rows (a one-photo bank) or tabletop photos are left out with the cause named; the closing line lists the files actually written, and an earlier fit's `fit_report.csv` is removed. New `fit --colour-mode` (ablation only) makes the mk / affine profiles.
- `check` and `export` refuse a profile measured at another size before any work (before, it surfaced only after every other method had run).
- Preflight: the batch copy is synchronised before the simulator is timed (on MPS too), and the loader's own rate (one image through the dataset on one thread, as a worker runs it, times the workers: an upper bound) is printed next to the step rate; the stage-1 estimate uses the slower of the two, or both in turn without workers. A two-view item costs a worker ~2x the Canon-only item (both views from the 576 px decode).

## [Unreleased] — 2026-10-30 Phone-camera simulator: tabletop photos re-rendered as handheld ones for training  (branch `retinareach`)

Why: the Canon model ranks phone photos well (AUROC 0.91-0.93 under every calibration) but at 0.85 sensitivity its specificity tops out near 0.79, while models that saw phone photos reach 0.86-0.89 (phone-ceiling seed 0). The gap is domain shift at the high-sensitivity end; this builds the tool to train it away without phone labels. REPORT.md §10.11.

- `phone_sim.py`: a stochastic imaging chain in the model's own view (after the loader's FOV crop and square resize), every parameter drawn from measurements of real, unlabelled handheld photos. Stages: `field` (undo the loader's ~20 % horizontal squash of Canon's truncated FOV circle, measured per image from the FOV's side boundaries; FOV-relative zoom from the disc-fovea distance; the minimum zoom that clears the truncated top and bottom, found by coverage; framing shift toward the detected optic disc, capped so the aperture never leaves the tabletop FOV; small roll; the phone's hard digital circular aperture), `light` (low-order illumination shape -- tilt, radial falloff, per-channel radial reddening -- transferred from a real phone photo; smooth random blotches at the measured 0.17-FOV-diameter scale; shadowed area capped at the drawn photo's), `colour` (green left alone; red and blue pulled toward green's structure -- the phone ISP's strongest fingerprint -- with a positive surrogate `(1-w) C + w mu_C (G/mu_G)^kappa`, w solved per image to the drawn photo's band-pass correlation; then ratio-preserving gains to its centre-zone means; all on the illumination-flattened image so the light pattern survives. Planted lesions keep x0.96-0.97 of their green contrast, real phones ~x1.1 (red and blue take on green's lesion signal: see 2026-10-31). `colour_mode: mk` (3x3 optimal transport) and `affine` stay for ablation: they close more of the statistical gap but make lesions 1.4-2x MORE conspicuous than on the tabletop photo, the wrong lesson for a model that will see fainter lesions on the phone), `bloom` (the detected disc pushed to the phone's blown-out disc colour; it reached 1.3 disc radii until 2026-10-31), `detail` (blur only, capped at 0.8 px, to the drawn photo's contrast-normalised band energy), `noise` (grain to the drawn photo's flat-region level, its spatial and cross-channel correlation calibrated by simulation in `fit`, tapered off on clipped and black pixels; after it, as part of `colour`, R and B fine structure, grain included, pulled toward G's to the drawn photo's correlation, as the phone's demosaic and colour matrix act after its sensor), `rim` (the drawn photo's rim haze crescents and dark bands, per sector and channel). Each simulated photo takes ONE real photo's whole row of measurements (the bank), so colour, light, sharpness, grain and rim stay consistent. The profile (JSON) holds statistics only, never pixels.
- `phone_sim.py fit`: the bank from the handheld TRAIN split of the trainer's own patient split (no label enters the bank, the split itself being stratified on referable DR; `--select icdr0` reads grades), the tabletop statistics from the source camera's train split, then a cross-fitted realism report (`fit_report.txt` / `.csv`: per statistic, AUROC of tabletop vs real handheld and of simulated vs real handheld, where a simulated photo is never scored against the photo its row came from) and `fit_grid.jpg`. `phone_sim.py render`: tabletop / simulated / real grid and a stage-by-stage strip.
- Rejected on the way, each on the samples, because it drew lesion-like structure or exaggerated lesions: whole-image colour moment matching (3x3 optimal transport 1.4-1.7x, per-channel 2x lesion contrast), per-channel histogram matching (haemorrhage-red blotches, purple foveal rings: BRSET's blue channel is nearly flat and stretching it amplifies texture), per-channel illumination gains (a magenta rim ring), a luminance-wide highlight curve (exudate-like white specks on rim reflections), unsharp masking (rings microaneurysms; the phone's extra high-frequency power is grain, not detail), blotches at half the measured scale (dark haemorrhage-sized patches).
- `train_retinareach.py --phone-sim PROFILE [--phone-sim-p 0.5] [--phone-sim-strength 1]`: in stage 1 each source training photo is re-rendered with probability p from a supersampled view (`supersample` x image size, so the zoom costs no detail) before the device-side augmentation, whose path it switches on. The preflight runs three steps through it and reports its time (mean of steps 2-3). Nothing changes without the flag.
- `style_transfer_xfer.py`: `phone_sim` method (`--phone-profile`, `--phone-strength`) in `check` and `export`, rendered from a supersampled view of the same rows; `score` adds `oracle_spec` (specificity where the test ROC crosses `--target-sens`, `train_phone_ceiling.py`'s reading). Fix: `check` and `score` now filter a `--source-camera` checkpoint's table before splitting it, as the trainer did -- before, a Canon-only checkpoint's "tabletop test" held Nikon photos and could hold its own training patients.
- `run_phone_sim.sh`: fit -> check (on `ck_rr_canon/seed<s>.pt`) -> the Canon sweep retrained with `--phone-sim` (via `run_retinareach.sh`, Nikon held out) -> score, Canon-only vs Canon + simulated phone photos on the same real test rows.
- Tests: `tests/test_phone_sim.py` (primitives; the squash reading; disc finder vs a rim reflection; zoom and shift keep the aperture inside a truncated field; profile round trip and guards; reproducibility, bounds and aperture; augment; no new lesion-scale spots on a clean fundus (without the field stage, so without the bloom); planted lesions keep their sign and >= 30 % of their contrast (one green pixel, on a phone-like fundus; replaced 2026-10-31); grain lands on the drawn level; fit / render CLI; trainer + check + score end to end).

## [Unreleased] — 2026-10-25 RetinaReach: round 3 of the calibration redesign; cross-fitted systemic probes  (branch `retinareach`)

What the 5-seed Canon sweep (2026-10-24) showed: Canon and the held-out Nikon meet the IDx-DR targets on every seed; the phone ranks well (AUROC 0.913, 0.926 after AdaBN) but fails at the shipped threshold, and full AdaBN cuts its per-patient sensitivity from 0.815 to 0.655; every systemic probe fails the gate, four of them UNDERPOWERED on the handheld test split. This round builds the fixes to test on those checkpoints, no retraining.

- `retinareach.label_free_threshold(q_captures, ref, method, q_reference=None)`: two new methods. `em2` runs EM where the Platt map holds — on the same captures scored under the source profile's statistics (`q_reference`) — and carries the posteriors to the calibrated scores, which set the threshold by expected sensitivity (EM on calibrated scores read the camera's score compression as "little disease here", REPORT 10.8). `rate` is rank-only: flag the top fraction `pi * target_sens + (1 - pi) * (1 - source_spec)` of the captures, immune to compression. `ThresholdReference.source_spec` (specificity at the shipped threshold on source validation) is fit and shipped for it; references written before it load with it unset and `rate` refuses them instead of guessing. `THRESHOLD_METHODS` = shipped, em, anchor, em2, rate. Without a reference, `em2` / `rate` reduce to `em`.
- `retinareach.prevalence_match_weights` / `prevalence_matched_sample`: the label-free prevalence-matched calibration pool. Experiment A showed the pool's disease rate, not the camera, drives AdaBN's sensitivity drop; the device cannot build a source-prevalence pool from labels, but it can from its own posteriors (EM on the source-statistics scores): weights `r * a + (1 - r)` with `a` solved so the pool's expected rate `sum(w r) / sum(w)` is the source's (or zero: predicted disease-free eyes only), then the calibration batches are resampled by those weights. `expected_prev` is the label-free estimate of the pool's rate the device can report; the lowest reachable rate is bounded by how soft the posteriors are.
- `redesign_calibration.py`: experiment **D** (the matched pool, source and zero prevalence, full depth and shallow, at the shipped threshold, with `matched_expected_prev` vs the labels' `matched_actual_prev`); B3 and C run every threshold method on every base, the matched pools included, with the pool under tabletop statistics as the `em2` / `rate` reference; C resamples the matched pool from the same N captures. The camera ladder gains the matched-pool, `shallow + EM2`, `shallow + rate` and `matched + shallow + EM2 / rate` columns and prints specificity beside sensitivity (both IDx-DR endpoints).
- `train_retinareach.py`: `operating_point.csv` and the calibration-size sweep report `em2` and `rate` (the pool and each capture draw are also scored under the source statistics as the reference; `flag_rate` recorded). `score_once.py` re-sets `em2` / `rate` the same way per fold, so `--primary calibrated:em2:<rule>` is available for the Messidor-2 look; a checkpoint whose references predate `source_spec` gets `rate` skipped with a note.
- Cross-fitted systemic probes (`probe_folds.py`; `train_retinareach.py --probe-folds K`, protocol `calibrated_oof<K>`): every probe fit K-fold out of fold over ALL handheld rows (patient-grouped; the trunk stays frozen and never sees a handheld label), C and the sensitivity threshold chosen by inner patient-grouped folds, permuted-label control per fold; the metadata comparator cross-fit on the same folds so the paired delta keeps its rows. On mBRSET that gates nephropathy, neuropathy, MI and smoking on 46 / 55 / 98 / 81 positive patients instead of 7-16. `probe_folds.py --ckpt <seed.pt> --target-root <mBRSET>` does it post hoc on a finished checkpoint and writes `capability_probes_oof.csv` + `predictions_<device>_probes_oof<K>.csv` next to the run; `summarize_retinareach.py` merges those rows into the capability table.
- Phone path: `export_retinareach.py` writes `threshold_reference` and a `calibration` spec (source profile, `shallow_layers` from `--shallow-frac`, default half the BN layers) into `retinareach_profiles.json`. `app/RetinaReachKit`: `calibrate(images, mode: .full | .shallow, thresholdMethod: .shipped | .em2 | .rate)` — shallow mode takes the first K layers' entries from the measured vector and the rest from the source profile (no new graph: the collector's first K entries are exactly those); `em2` / `rate` score the captures under the source profile and under the calibrated statistics and re-set each source target's threshold with the same arithmetic as Python; `screen` flags at the stored threshold when the calibration carries one. `retinareach-cli calibrate --mode shallow --threshold em2`. Defaults unchanged (full, shipped), so the parity check is unaffected.
- Tests: em2 / rate under score compression, the matched pool's rate and its label-free estimate, `cross_fit_probe` (every patient once, both eyes in one fold, controls at chance), experiment D and the five methods in the redesign run, the post-hoc probe folds and their pickup by the summarizer, the in-line `--probe-folds` run, `rate` skipped for a pre-`source_spec` reference in `score_once.py`.
